# Voice & Tone — DCC Care

## Principles

1. **Plain and humane.** The system handles students and discipline; copy stays
   calm, respectful, and free of bureaucracy-speak. "File an offense" not
   "Initiate a disciplinary incident record".
2. **The record is the product.** Case numbers, dates, and IDs are content, not
   chrome. Always render them faithfully in monospace; never round, abbreviate,
   or decorate them.
3. **Institutional, not corporate.** Confident, official, warm. The voice of a
   well-run discipline office — firm but fair.

## Mechanics

- Sentence case for everything except page titles and proper nouns.
- Buttons: verb-first, ≤ 3 words ("Add student", "File offense", "Scan card").
- Empty states: one reassuring sentence + one action ("No offenses on record yet — a clean slate. File the first one if needed.").
- Errors: state what happened and the fix ("That RFID is already assigned to Dela Cruz, M.").
- No emoji, no exclamation marks in UI copy, no "Oops".
- Dates: "Oct 22, 2026" in tables; "October 22, 2026" in formal records.
- Times: 12-hour with lowercase am/pm ("7:42 am").
